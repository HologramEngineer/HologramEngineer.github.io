About me

I'm a Unity gameplay, UI and tools engineer with 10 years of commercial software experience, based in Central Europe (Slovakia). I hold a master's degree from Technical University of Košice in Intelligent systems, focused on robotics and artificial intelligence.

From 07/2016 to 09/2026 I worked at Inlogic Software, a Slovakia-based mobile games studio, as a general game developer - owning gameplay programming, UI, VFX, performance optimization, third-party SDK integration, ad services, in-app purchases, and later backend/multiplayer architecture for a live-service title. I worked as a solo engineer alongside game and graphics designers on most projects, and every title I shipped was released commercially on Google Play, the App Store, or sold to third-party publishers.

While my background is in games, the underlying work translates directly to software engineering more broadly: C# architecture and performance optimization, cloud/backend integration (Azure, PlayFab), systems design under technical constraints (memory, framerate, deterministic physics), and shipping and maintaining live products used by hundreds of thousands of people. I'm now looking for a full-time role where I can apply that either in games or in general software development.
